How it works

Real roads

Courses are real public roads with a start gate, a finish gate and a reference benchmark — never a fabricated human record.

Scored on the server

Your phone shows a provisional score; the server recomputes every run independently. The server's number is the one that counts.

Verified, or not ranked

Runs that can't be verified are kept, scored and shown to you — and ranked nowhere. Nobody is accused of anything.

Race a ghost

A ghost is the shape of a drive over time, not a GPS trace. You see a live gap to the driver you're chasing — including yourself on a better day — and nobody ever sees anyone's route.

Drive safe. Every time.

This is not a racing game. Never interact with the app while moving. Mount your phone before you start. Follow every traffic law. The goal is smooth, controlled driving — and the scoring is built so that reckless driving loses.
